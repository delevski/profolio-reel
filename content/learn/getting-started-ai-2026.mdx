The fastest path to AI fluency isn't another video course — it's **shipping small projects with an agent at your side**.

## Week 1: Build something tiny

Pick a problem you actually have. Use an agent to scaffold, implement, and test. Document what surprised you.

## Week 2: Add evaluation

Measure outputs — latency, cost, accuracy. Agents without metrics are demos, not products.

## Week 3: Share and iterate

Write a short post or thread. Teaching solidifies learning and surfaces gaps in your mental model.
