Midjourney V7 rewards structured prompts over vague descriptions.

## Template structure

```
[subject], [style reference], [lighting], [camera], --ar 16:9 --v 7
```

## Style references

Use `--sref` with curated mood boards for consistent brand visuals across campaigns.

## Common mistakes

Avoid stacking contradictory adjectives. One clear subject beats five vague modifiers.
